Agentic commerce infrastructure

Make a product visible, explainable, and measurable inside AI shopping.

This prototype does not claim to secretly rank a brand inside OpenAI or Anthropic. It shows the actual work needed: create agent-readable product assets, prepare provider payloads, and measure whether the brand is mentioned in relevant shopping answers.

Try the walkthroughSee the KPI

What it achieves

A brand gets a distribution package agents can ingest or cite.

  • Product passport: buyer fit, use cases, proof, caveats, price, inventory, and returns.
  • Provider payloads: feed fields, JSON-LD, OpenAI-style product data, and Claude/MCP catalog response.
  • Measurement: estimated and then live-tracked Agent Share of Answers across relevant prompts.
