Agentic commerce infrastructure

Make emerging brands readable to AI shopping agents.

Agentic Shelf is a guided onboarding layer that turns ordinary product knowledge into structured product passports, distribution readiness, and shopping-intent visibility tests.

Try the walkthroughSee the process

Context

Consumer discovery is moving from search pages to AI answers.

  • New brands need clean product facts, proof, policies, and purchase options that agents can trust.
  • Agents need structured evidence before recommending unfamiliar products over established brands.
  • This product acts like an onboarding and distribution layer between brands and agentic shopping surfaces.

How to use it

A four-step flow for nontechnical brand teams.

Open demo
01

Answer plain questions

Describe the product, target buyer, use cases, claims, proof, price, inventory, and returns.

02

Create a product passport

The system converts the answers into a structured truth layer that agents can query or ingest.

03

Map distribution rails

Readiness is checked for OpenAI, Google, Shopify, Perplexity, Claude/MCP, and marketplace feeds.

04

Monitor visibility

Prompt simulations show where the product should appear and what evidence is still missing.

What this achieves
It makes the brand easier to discover, recommend, and buy through agents.

        

          
For brands
No protocol knowledge required. The system tells operators what to provide and what is missing.

          
For agents
Agents receive structured, current, and evidence-backed product data instead of scraping vague pages.

          
For commerce
Recommendations become more confident because price, inventory, policies, and proof are easier to verify.
