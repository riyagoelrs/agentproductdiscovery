Process

How it works

Four steps from invisible to recommended.

Step 1

Answer the brand questionnaire

Tell us who your buyer is, what shopping queries you want to win, what makes you different, and what proof backs your claims. More detail = higher score.

Step 2

Get your AI-ready asset pack

We generate a product passport, schema.org JSON-LD to embed in your site, a provider-ready product feed row, and an MCP tool response shape for Claude integration.

Step 3

Complete per-platform action items

Each AI shopping platform has specific submission requirements. We give you a step-by-step checklist for Google, OpenAI/Bing, Perplexity, Claude, and Shopify.

Step 4

Measure your AI visibility lift

Your dashboard tracks AI Visibility Score over time. Once you add API keys, we run real daily queries across AI engines and show your actual mention rate per keyword.

Generated assets

      
What we build for you

      
Six assets, generated from your answers, formatted for each AI platform.

      

        

          
📋

          
Product passport

          
Normalized summary of your brand, buyer fit, use cases, differentiators, proof, pricing, and policies — the source of truth for all other assets.

        

        

          
📦

          
Provider payload

          
A product feed row formatted for Google Merchant Center, Microsoft Merchant Center, and Perplexity — ready to upload.

        

        

          
🏷️

          
JSON-LD schema.org markup

          
Drop into your product page <head>. Includes Product, AggregateRating, Offer, and Brand — everything AI crawlers parse.

        

        

          
🤖

          
MCP tool response

          
Anthropic Model Context Protocol response shape — what your product looks like when a Claude shopping agent retrieves it via tool call.

        

        

          
🔍

          
Shopping intent index

          
Your target queries ranked by projected mention lift — tells you which keywords to win first and where to focus proof points.

        

        

          
📈

          
AI Visibility Score

          
A 0–100 composite score across profile completeness, proof quality, structured data, and platform action completion. Tracked daily.
